    What changed between the consultation draft (Nov 2025) and the final Guidelines (7 Oct 2026)¶

    Sources: consultation paper P017-2025 (docs/sources/mas_consultation_paper_P017-2025_2025-11-13.pdf, Section 6 "Proposed Guidelines" and Annex), final Guidelines (docs/sources/mas_airm_guidelines_2026-10-07.pdf) and MAS' Response to Feedback (docs/sources/mas_response_to_feedback_P017-2025_2026-10-07.pdf, cited as Resp §n). Paragraph numbers: D-x.y = draft, §x.y = final. The draft had five sections; the final inserts a new Section 2, so draft Sections 2–5 became final Sections 3–6.

    Legend: T tightened (new or stricter expectation) · E eased (relaxed, made optional or proportionate) · R removed · C clarified / restructured without changing substance · N new.

    3. Net assessment¶

    • Net direction: eased on process, tightened on substance for newer AI. The final text removes most prescriptive organisational mechanics (dedicated committee, attestation processes, annual reviews, "all outputs reviewed", aggregate portfolio reviews, "full range" of data conditions) and replaces them with outcome language plus proportionality. At the same time it adds concrete obligations that did not exist in the draft: embedded-AI identification for material providers, shadow-AI residual-risk management, agent inventory attributes, guardrail-effectiveness testing, GenAI/agent logging, local senior management accountability, vendor limit/suspend/replace, and rejection of vendor self-attestations.
    • For LLM and agentic systems specifically, the binding deltas are §4.4/fn22, fn25, §5.9(c)(d), §5.11(a)(d), §5.15/fn46, §5.23(a)(d) and §5.25(a). These are the clauses the toolkit's genai and agent tags key on.
    • Nothing in the Response to Feedback prescribes metrics, thresholds, or a formula for combining Impact / Complexity / Reliance (Resp §7.19, §10.28–10.29, §10.66). Every quantitative default in this toolkit is therefore the authors' proposal, not MAS'.
