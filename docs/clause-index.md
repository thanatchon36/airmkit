## Expectations with no implementing control

- **§2.2** (Application of the Guidelines) — Unless 2.3 applies, an FI must establish oversight frameworks with AI identification, materiality assessment and inventory (Sections 3-4) and apply lifecycle controls and capabilities (Sections 5-6), calibrated by relevance and risk materiality.
