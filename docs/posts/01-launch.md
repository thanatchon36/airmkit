The MAS Guidelines on AI Risk Management bind from 7 October 2027. We built an open-source toolkit that turns them into machine-checkable controls, then measured how far that actually works.

In the box:
- A machine-readable catalogue: 65 paragraphs, 51 expectations, 29 footnotes that carry requirements (the fn25 agent attributes among them).
- 65 controls, each citing the paragraph it serves and labelled by how far it can be automated.
- A 224-row crosswalk to ISO/IEC 42001, NIST AI RMF and its GenAI Profile, the EU AI Act, FEAT and the 2024 MAS Information Paper.
- A worked example on a fictional bank with 8 synthetic use cases, and five pre-registered experiments.

The headline: 45.1% of expectations admit a fully automated check. Section 4 (inventory, materiality) is 84.6% automatable; Section 3 (governance) is 0.0%.

Of 17 hypotheses fixed before the experiments ran, 7 were refuted. All of them are in the paper, with the thresholds they failed.
