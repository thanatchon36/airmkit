We built an open-source toolkit for the MAS Guidelines on AI Risk Management (7 Oct 2026), and measured how far such guidance can actually be automated.

What is in it:
- A machine-readable clause catalogue: 65 paragraphs, 51 expectations, 29 footnotes that carry requirements (including the fn25 agent attributes).
- 65 controls, each citing the paragraph it serves, each labelled by how far it can be automated.
- A crosswalk to ISO/IEC 42001, NIST AI RMF + GenAI Profile, EU AI Act, FEAT and the 2024 MAS Information Paper: 224 rows.
- A worked example on a fictional bank with 8 synthetic use cases, and five pre-registered experiments.

The headline: 45.1% of expectations admit a fully automated check. Section 4 (inventory, materiality) is 84.6% automatable. Section 3 (governance) is 0.0%.

Of 17 hypotheses fixed before the experiments ran, 7 were refuted. Those are in the repo too.
