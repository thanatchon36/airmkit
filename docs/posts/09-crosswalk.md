If you hold ISO/IEC 42001 and align to the NIST AI RMF, how much of the MAS AI Risk Management Guidelines is already done?

We hand-authored 224 mapping rows over 51 expectations, counting an unmapped expectation against coverage rather than excluding it.

- ISO/IEC 42001: 37.2% fully covered
- NIST AI RMF: 49.0%
- EU AI Act: 43.1%
- NIST GenAI Profile: 29.4% (narrow, but accurate where it applies)
- Union of ISO + NIST + EU AI Act: 64.7%, leaving **35.3% with no full mapping**

We pre-registered a prediction that the residue would be the agent and generative-AI clauses. **It was refuted.** Those are covered. What no framework fully covers is governance machinery and MAS-specific proportionality: the applicability test and basic regime (§2.2, §2.5), board responsibilities (§3.4), identification and shadow AI (§4.1, §4.4), materiality arbitration (§4.13), third-party accountability (§5.11), independent validation scope (§5.20).

Mapping is interpretive and asymmetric - a management-system standard, product law and voluntary guidance are not commensurable - so 18 rows are flagged low confidence and listed individually.
