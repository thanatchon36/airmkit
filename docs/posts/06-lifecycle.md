One detail changes how you should read any AI control report, including ours.

In the Section 5 lifecycle clauses of the MAS Guidelines, most expectations are not "does the artefact exist" but "is the artefact adequate". So in our catalogue such a control is labelled semi: a machine confirms the artefact exists and is current; a human judges adequacy.

A semi control therefore never reports PASS. When its automated check succeeds, the outcome is NEEDS_ATTESTATION.

On the fictional portfolio that means 277 checks need attestation and 30 are genuine automated failures. Read the first number as failures and you will panic; read it as a work queue and it is the honest answer.

Counted per control, semi is the largest class (36.9%), ahead of auto (30.8%) and doc (32.3%). And 93.1% of estimated compliance effort sits in the semi and doc classes - the part no tool finishes for you.

Section 5 binds from 7 Oct 2028. The work is mostly people.
