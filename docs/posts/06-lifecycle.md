A detail that changes how you read any AI control report, including ours.

In the MAS Guidelines' Section 5 lifecycle clauses, most expectations are not "does the artefact exist" but "is the artefact adequate". So in our catalogue a control is labelled `semi`: a tool can confirm the artefact exists and is current, and a human must judge adequacy.

A `semi` control therefore **never reports PASS**. When its automated check succeeds, the outcome is NEEDS_ATTESTATION.

On the fictional portfolio that means 277 checks need attestation and 30 are genuine automated failures. Read the first number as a failure count and you will panic; read it as a work queue and it is the honest answer.

Counted per control, `semi` is the largest class (36.9%), ahead of `auto` (30.8%) and `doc` (32.3%). And 93.1% of estimated compliance effort sits in the semi and doc classes - the part no tool finishes for you.

Section 5 takes effect 7 Oct 2028.
