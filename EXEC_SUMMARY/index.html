    MAS AI Risk Management Guidelines: what they require, what a tool can check, what still needs people¶

    For CROs and Heads of Model Risk. Every number is generated from results/summary.json; nothing here is typed by hand. Generated 2026-10-13 from airmkit 0.1.0 (2481930).

    airmkit is an independent open-source project, not affiliated with or endorsed by the Monetary Authority of Singapore. It is not legal, regulatory or compliance advice and is not a substitute for reading the Guidelines on Artificial Intelligence Risk Management (7 October 2026). Interpretations and all thresholds are the authors'.

    The deadlines¶

    MAS issued the Guidelines on 7 October 2026, phased (§1.8): Sections 3-4 by 7 Oct 2027 - governance and control functions, AI identification including embedded third-party AI (§4.2, fn21), the inventory with agent attributes (fn25), use-case materiality over Impact, Complexity and Reliance (§4.12), and residual risk from unidentified AI kept within appetite (§4.4, fn22). Sections 5-6 by 7 Oct 2028 - the lifecycle controls and the competence of staff using AI (§6.1), with high-risk lifecycle controls expected sooner.

    Two things to plan around. MAS prescribes no formula, metric or threshold for materiality, performance or guardrails (Resp §7.19, §10.29): every number you use is yours to defend. And the final text, while more proportionate than the draft, is tighter on third-party AI and on generative AI and agents: you retain primary accountability and must be able to limit, suspend or replace a provider (§5.11), vendor self-attestation generally does not close a transparency gap (Resp §9.9), and guardrails must be tested for effectiveness (§5.15, fn46).

    What tooling covers¶

    Of 51 expectation paragraphs, 45.1% admit a fully automated check, 31.4% are semi-automatable (a tool confirms the artefact exists and is current; a person judges adequacy) and 23.5% are documentary. The distribution matters more: Section 4 is 84.6% automatable, Section 3 is 0.0%. Tooling can carry the 2027 obligations and much less of the 2028 ones. On our 8-use-case fictional portfolio, 93.1% of estimated effort is attestation and documentation against 6.8% a tool finishes unaided, spread evenly across use cases (Gini 0.0897). Headcount, not tooling, is the binding constraint. (Hours are our estimates from a published table, not measurements.)

    Four findings worth acting on¶

    1. A system becoming an agent raises the control set, not the tier. 7 controls first bind at the agent step - guardrail effectiveness testing, tool permissions, trace monitoring - but only 1 of 4 use cases gained a tier, because tier is dominated by consequence. Review the control set, not the label.
    2. Guardrail tests against attacks only will mislead you. Attack success fell from 75.0% unguarded to 6.2% layered on a local model - and the same layered defence refused 50.0% of benign in-scope requests. Against our pre-registered joint criterion, 0 of 5 patterns passed. Require a benign control set in every guardrail report you accept, internal or vendor.
    3. ISO 42001 and NIST alignment leaves governance work, not agent work. No framework fully covers more than 49.0% of expectations; the ISO + NIST + EU AI Act union leaves 35.3% with no full mapping. The residue is the applicability test and basic regime (§2.2, §2.5), board responsibilities (§3.4), identification and shadow AI (§4.1, §4.4), materiality arbitration (§4.13), third-party accountability (§5.11) and independent validation scope (§5.20) - not the agent clauses, which the NIST Generative AI Profile covers well. If you are certified, that list is your remaining work.
    4. Proportionality scales rigour, not volume. Critical-tier use cases need 91.4 evidence items on average against 92.0 for Medium. The same artefacts are required; what changes is the depth of review and who signs.

    Next quarter¶

    Build the inventory first - it is the 2027 obligation, the most automatable, and everything else depends on it. Write down your materiality method and the reason behind each signal, since MAS will not supply one. Run a shadow-AI sweep and record the residual-risk decision, not only the findings. Change your guardrail test template to report over-refusal beside attack success. Re-scope third-party assessments to the eight §5.11 considerations.

    Reproduce or dispute it¶

    pip install airmkit
airm clauses                                # clause catalogue with effective dates
airm assess -r ai-inventory.yaml            # materiality, with reasons for every score
airm check  -r ai-inventory.yaml            # pass / fail / needs-attestation / not-applicable
airm report -r ai-inventory.yaml -o pack/   # the full audit pack

    Every control cites the paragraph it serves and every automatability class carries a written justification, so one judgement can be disputed without rejecting the method. The worked example, the five experiments, the pre-registered thresholds and all 7 refuted hypotheses are in the repository: https://github.com/thanatchon36/airmkit
