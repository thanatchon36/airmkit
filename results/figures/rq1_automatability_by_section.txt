Automatability of MAS-AIRM expectation paragraphs by section, as implemented by this toolkit. auto: a deterministic function of the inventory entry and its evidence decides pass/fail. semi: presence and currency of the artefact is machine-checkable but adequacy needs human attestation. doc: organisational or judgemental, with no artefact the toolkit inspects. Classification is the authors' judgement relative to this implementation, not a MAS classification.
