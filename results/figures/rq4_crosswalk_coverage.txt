Coverage of the 51 MAS-AIRM expectation paragraphs by other AI governance frameworks. full: the other framework imposes an equivalent obligation. partial: related but narrower, differently scoped or optional. none: no mapping asserted. Mapping is interpretive and asymmetric, since ISO/IEC 42001 is a management-system standard, the EU AI Act is product law and the NIST AI RMF is voluntary guidance, so a paragraph can be covered in substance and uncovered in legal effect.
